FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Nova?
Nova is a design system platform that combines AI-powered personalization with accessible, high-performance components. It helps teams ship beautiful interfaces faster while maintaining consistency across all platforms.
How does AI personalization work?
Nova analyzes your brand assets, design history, and usage patterns to generate components that match your identity. The AI continuously learns from your team's feedback and adapts the design system accordingly.
Is Nova accessible?
Yes. Every component in Nova is built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We test with screen readers, ensure keyboard-first interactions, and provide visible focus states. Accessibility is a core requirement, not an afterthought.
Can I self-host Nova?
Yes, self-hosting is available on our Enterprise plan. We provide Docker images, Kubernetes manifests, and full documentation to help you deploy Nova in your own infrastructure.
What support do you offer?
Starter plans include community support via our Discord and GitHub. Pro plans include priority email support with a 4-hour response time. Enterprise plans include a dedicated support engineer and custom SLAs.
