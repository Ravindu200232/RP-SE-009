FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does Agentic differ from chatbots?

Chatbots respond to prompts. Agentic agents execute tasks end-to-end — they plan, use tools, make decisions, and deliver complete work products. You delegate outcomes, not conversations.

What tasks can agents perform?

Agents can handle research, report generation, email drafting, data entry, scheduling, social media management, code review, customer follow-ups, and hundreds of other workflows. If it involves digital tools, your agent can likely do it.

How long does it take to set up?

Most users have their first agent running in under 10 minutes. Connect your tools, describe your workflow, and you're live. No code required.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your data is never used to train models for other customers.

Can agents work with my existing tools?

Absolutely. We integrate with Slack, GitHub, Notion, Google Workspace, Salesforce, HubSpot, and 200+ other tools. If there's an API, we can connect to it.

What happens if an agent gets stuck?

Agents are designed to recognize when they need help. They'll pause and ask for guidance, or escalate to a human. You're always in control.
