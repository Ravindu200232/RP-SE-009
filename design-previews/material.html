Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

What is Lumina?

Lumina is a design analytics platform that helps teams track component usage, token adoption, and design system health in real time.

How does pricing work?

We offer a free Starter plan for small teams, a Pro plan at $49/month for growing teams, and custom Enterprise pricing for large organizations.

Can I migrate from other tools?

Yes! We provide migration guides and import tools for popular design system platforms. Most teams migrate in under a day.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use SOC 2 Type II compliant infrastructure, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and never sell your data.

Do you offer support?

Yes. Starter plans get community support, Pro plans get priority email support, and Enterprise plans get dedicated support with SLAs.
