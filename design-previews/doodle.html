Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start doodling.

Is Doodle really free to start?

Yes! Our Doodler plan is free forever. No credit card required. You can upgrade anytime when you're ready for more features.

Can I migrate from other tools?

Absolutely! We offer one-click importers for Trello, Asana, and Jira. Your data will be migrated in minutes, not hours.

How secure is my data?

We use 256-bit encryption at rest and in transit. Our infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

We love startups! Eligible early-stage companies get 50% off Pro for the first year. Just email us at hello@doodle.app.

What happens if I exceed my plan limits?

We'll notify you before you hit any limits. You can upgrade instantly or we'll help you find a plan that fits your needs.
