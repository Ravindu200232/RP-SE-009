Why artistic?

Every decision is intentional. We trade decoration for clarity, and the result is an interface that feels both bold and accessible.

  • Accessible by default — AA contrast, focus-visible, reduced motion.
  • Consistent rhythm — 4px spacing scale, mono type.
  • No surprises — sharp corners, explicit states.

FAQ

Why no rounded corners?

Sharp corners are part of the artistic identity — they create a distinctive, high-contrast visual rhythm that aligns with our accessibility-first approach.

Can I use your tokens in my project?

Absolutely. The system is MIT licensed, and all tokens are documented in the style guide.

Is it really WCAG 2.2 AA?

Yes. Every component is tested with automated and manual checks. We maintain a 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum for normal text.
