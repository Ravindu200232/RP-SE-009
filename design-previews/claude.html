FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is Claude different from other AI assistants?

Claude is built by Anthropic with a focus on safety, interpretability, and long-context understanding. It's designed to be genuinely helpful, harmless, and honest — with a 200K token context window that lets it process entire documents in one pass.

What is the context window?

The context window is the amount of text Claude can consider at once. With 200K tokens, Claude can read and reason about entire books, lengthy research papers, or large codebases — and reference any part of them in its responses.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Claude is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA eligible. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is never used to train models without explicit permission. Enterprise plans include data residency options.

Can I use Claude for commercial purposes?

Absolutely. Claude is designed for commercial use. The Pro and Team plans include commercial usage rights, and the API allows you to build and deploy Claude-powered applications at scale.

What integrations are available?

Claude works in your browser, via the API, and through integrations with Slack, Google Docs, and other productivity tools. The API supports tool use, letting Claude interact with your own systems and databases.
