Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about shadcn.

Is shadcn free to use?

Yes, the core components are completely free and open source. We offer paid plans for advanced features and support.

Can I customize the design tokens?

Absolutely! All tokens are CSS variables that you can override. This makes it easy to match your brand while keeping consistency.

Is it accessible out of the box?

Yes, every component is built with accessibility in mind. We follow WCAG 2.2 AA standards and test with screen readers.

What about browser support?

We support all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. No IE support.

Can I use it with my framework?

Yes! We have adapters for React, Vue, Svelte, and vanilla HTML/CSS. The core design tokens work everywhere.
