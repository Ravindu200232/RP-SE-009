FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is perspective design?

It's a spatial design approach using isometric grids, vanishing points, and layered elements to create depth and guide attention.

Is it accessible?

Yes, we follow WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines with keyboard-first interactions and visible focus states.

Can I use it with my existing design system?

Absolutely. Our tokens and components are designed to be layered onto existing systems.

Do you offer migration support?

Yes, our enterprise plan includes dedicated migration assistance from our team.
