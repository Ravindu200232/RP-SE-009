Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Claymorphic.

What exactly is claymorphism?

Claymorphism is a UI design trend characterized by soft, rounded, 3D-like shapes that mimic malleable clay. It uses puffy elements, soft shadows, and colorful surfaces to create playful, tactile interfaces.

Is Claymorphic accessible?

Yes! Every component meets WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We test contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, focus states, and screen reader compatibility on every release.

Can I customize the tokens?

Absolutely. All colors, spacing, typography, and shadows are exposed as CSS custom properties. You can override them to match your brand while maintaining the claymorphic aesthetic.

What frameworks are supported?

Claymorphic is framework-agnostic. We provide vanilla CSS, plus official packages for React, Vue, and Svelte. Tailwind CSS integration is also available.

How do I migrate from another design system?

We provide a migration guide and codemods for common patterns. Start by replacing your color tokens, then spacing, then components. Our CLI can automate most of the process.
