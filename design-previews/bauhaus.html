What We Build

Geometric UI

Every interface element is constructed from pure geometric forms — circles, squares, and triangles — creating visual harmony through mathematical precision.

Primary Palette

A strictly limited color system of Bauhaus primaries — red, blue, and yellow — delivers maximum impact with minimal visual noise.

Hard Shadows

Depth is created through offset shadows — never soft or blurred — giving every element a crisp, printed, poster-like quality.
