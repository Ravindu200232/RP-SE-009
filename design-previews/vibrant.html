// faq

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know.

Is Vibrant really accessible?

Yes! We're WCAG 2.2 AA compliant and test every component with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, and automated tools. Accessibility is our foundation, not an afterthought.

Can I customize the design tokens?

Absolutely. Every color, spacing, and font token is exposed as a CSS variable. You can override them globally or per-component. Your brand, your rules.

What about reduced motion preferences?

We respect prefers-reduced-motion out of the box. All animations gracefully degrade to static states, and transitions are minimized for users who need it.

Do you support dark mode?

Yes, dark mode is built in. Our semantic tokens automatically adjust for contrast in both light and dark themes, so you never have to worry about readability.

How do I migrate from another design system?

We provide a migration guide and codemods that automate most of the work. Most teams complete the switch in under a week, with full support from our docs and community.
