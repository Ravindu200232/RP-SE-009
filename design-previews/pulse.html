FAQ

Can I use Pulse with my existing stack?

Yes — it's token‑based and framework‑agnostic. Use the CSS variables anywhere.

Are the borders always 4px?

Yes, that's the signature. Structural elements use 4px borders; interactive states keep the same weight.

How do you handle accessibility with such bold colors?

We maintain a contrast‑safe palette: #EA580B on #FFEDD5 passes AA for normal text. Focus states use thick outlines.

Can I customize the motion?

Yes, spring curves are exposed as tokens. But we recommend keeping them snappy — no slow fades.
