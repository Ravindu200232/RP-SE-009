FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Start a 14-day free trial on any plan. No credit card required. You'll get full access to all features, and you can cancel anytime.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes are prorated automatically, and you'll see the difference on your next invoice.

How secure is my data?

We're SOC 2 Type II compliant. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we offer SSO, RBAC, and audit logs on enterprise plans.

Do you offer discounts for startups?

Yes! We offer 50% off for early-stage startups through our accelerator program. Reach out to us to learn more.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

We'll notify you when you reach 80% of your limit. If you exceed it, we'll automatically upgrade you to the next tier and prorate the difference.
