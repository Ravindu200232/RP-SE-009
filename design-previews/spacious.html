FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Spacious.

What is Spacious?

Spacious is a design system built on generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, and calm interfaces. It provides design tokens, components, and guidelines to help teams build consistent, accessible, and beautiful products.

How does the 8pt grid work?

The 8pt grid uses 8-pixel increments for all spacing, padding, and layout measurements. This creates a consistent visual rhythm across your interface, making it easier to align elements and maintain harmony at every breakpoint.

Can I use Spacious with my existing design tools?

Yes. Spacious provides design tokens that export to Figma, Sketch, and Adobe XD, plus a Figma plugin for seamless syncing. On the development side, tokens work with CSS, Tailwind, and styled-components.

Is Spacious accessible?

Absolutely. Every component is built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards, including keyboard navigation, visible focus states, screen-reader compatibility, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility is a core requirement, not an afterthought.

How do I migrate from my current design system?

We provide a migration guide that walks you through the process step by step. Our team can also assist with automated token mapping and component replacement to minimize disruption to your workflow.

Do you offer enterprise support?

Yes. Enterprise plans include dedicated support, SSO/SAML authentication, custom theming, SLA guarantees, and priority access to our engineering team for any questions or issues.
