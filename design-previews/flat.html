FEATURES

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Clean Typography

Inter at every weight, sized on a strict 12–32px scale for perfect hierarchy.

Accessible by Default

WCAG 2.2 AA compliant with keyboard-first interactions and visible focus states.

Fast Performance

No shadows, no gradients, no bloat. Just lightweight, semantic CSS that loads instantly.

Design Tokens

Semantic tokens for color, spacing, and type. Change one value, update the entire system.

Responsive Grid

A fluid 12-column grid that works from 400px to 4K. Built for every screen.

Keyboard-First

Every component is fully navigable by keyboard. No mouse required, no exceptions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Flux really free?
Yes, the Starter plan is free forever with no credit card required. You get 5 projects and 50 components, which is plenty for personal projects and small prototypes.
Can I use Flux with my existing codebase?
Absolutely. Flux works with any framework and can be adopted incrementally. You can start with just a few components and gradually migrate your entire UI.
Does Flux support dark mode?
Yes, Flux includes dark mode support with semantic tokens. Switch between light and dark by toggling a single attribute on the root element.
How often is Flux updated?
We ship updates every two weeks with new components, bug fixes, and improvements. Breaking changes are announced at least one release in advance.
Do you offer enterprise support?
Yes, our Enterprise plan includes dedicated support with a 4-hour response time, SSO/SAML, custom contracts, and an SLA guarantee.
