FAQ

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Everything you need to know about Cosmic.

What is Cosmic?
Cosmic is a futuristic design system for building immersive, spatial digital experiences. It includes a complete component library, design tokens, and theming tools.
Can I use it for free?
Yes! The Starter plan is free forever and includes 5 components, basic themes, and community support. Upgrade to Pro for the full experience.
Does it support dark mode?
Dark mode is the default in Cosmic. Every component is designed dark-first with neon accent support. Light mode is also available for specific use cases.
Is it accessible?
Absolutely. Cosmic is WCAG 2.2 AA compliant by default. Every component includes proper ARIA attributes, keyboard navigation, and visible focus states.
Can I customize it?
Yes! All design tokens are fully customizable. Change colors, spacing, typography, and more. Your custom theme can be exported and shared.
Do you offer support?
Pro and Enterprise plans include priority support. Enterprise customers get a dedicated support engineer and custom SLAs.
