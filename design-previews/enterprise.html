Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Enterprise.

How does the free trial work?

Start your 14-day free trial on the Pro plan with full access to all features. No credit card required. After the trial, you can choose to continue with a paid plan or downgrade to the free Starter plan.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include access to our documentation and community forum. Pro plans include priority email support with a 4-hour response time. Enterprise plans include 24/7 dedicated support with a 1-hour response time.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We're SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We offer SSO, SAML, and detailed audit logs on Enterprise plans.

Can I migrate from another platform?

Yes! We provide comprehensive migration guides and tools for popular platforms like AWS, Heroku, and Vercel. Our support team can also assist with complex migrations on Enterprise plans.
