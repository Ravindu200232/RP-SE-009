FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Sketch free to use?

Yes! The Hobby plan is free forever. You get access to all components with up to 3 projects. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited projects and advanced features.

Can I customize the design tokens?

Absolutely. Every color, spacing value, and font weight is exposed as a semantic token. You can override them in your Tailwind config or CSS variables.

Is Sketch accessible?

Yes, every component is built to WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We test with screen readers, keyboard navigation, and color contrast tools.

What frameworks do you support?

Sketch works with any framework that supports CSS and HTML. We provide official integrations for React, Vue, and Svelte, plus vanilla HTML/CSS.
