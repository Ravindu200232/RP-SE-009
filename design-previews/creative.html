Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know. Can't find an answer? Contact us!

Is there a free trial?
Yes! All plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You can cancel anytime during the trial period.
Can I switch plans later?
Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate the difference.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Yes! We offer a 50% discount for registered non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Contact our support team to get started.
What happens to my projects if I cancel?
Your projects remain accessible in read-only mode for 30 days after cancellation. You can export everything before then. After 30 days, projects are permanently deleted.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. We're also SOC 2 Type II certified.
