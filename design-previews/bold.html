FAQ — bold answers

Why no rounded corners?

Sharp corners are part of the bold identity — they communicate precision and confidence. They also make focus states more distinguishable.

Is Archivo Black the only font?

Yes, for display. JetBrains Mono is used for labels, code, and data. Both are loaded via Google Fonts.

What about accessibility?

We meet WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere. High contrast is built into the token set, and focus is always visible.
