FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Square?

Square is a comprehensive design system that provides tokens, components, and guidelines to help teams build consistent, accessible products faster.

How does pricing work?

Start with the free Starter plan. Upgrade to Pro for $29/month when you need unlimited projects and advanced features. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Can I use Square with my existing stack?

Yes. Square is framework-agnostic and works with React, Vue, Angular, or vanilla HTML/CSS. Our tokens integrate with any styling solution.

Is Square accessible?

Absolutely. Every component is built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards, with keyboard-first interactions and visible focus states.

Do you offer support?

Yes. Starter includes community support, Pro includes priority email support, and Enterprise includes dedicated support with custom SLAs.
