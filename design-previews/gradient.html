Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Gradient.

Is Gradient free to use?
Yes! The Starter plan is completely free with an MIT license. You can use it in personal and commercial projects without any restrictions.
Can I customize the design tokens?
Absolutely! All design tokens are fully customizable. You can override colors, typography, spacing, and more to match your brand perfectly.
Is the design system accessible?
Yes, every component is built to WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We test for keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and color contrast.
Do you offer a Figma library?
Yes! Pro and Enterprise plans include access to our complete Figma library with all components, tokens, and design assets.
What frameworks are supported?
Gradient works with React, Vue, Svelte, and vanilla JavaScript. We also provide framework-specific wrappers for the best developer experience.
