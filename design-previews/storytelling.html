✦ Chapter Seven — The Q&A

Frequently asked questions

What is Storyline?
Storyline is a platform that helps brands craft narrative-driven digital experiences. We provide the tools, frameworks, and guidance to turn your website into a compelling story that connects with your audience.
How does the storytelling approach work?
We use proven narrative frameworks — like the hero's journey and the three-act structure — to organize your content into a cohesive story. This makes your website feel more engaging and guides users naturally toward conversion.
Can I migrate from my current platform?
Absolutely. We offer migration tools and dedicated support to help you move your existing content seamlessly. Most teams are fully migrated within a week.
Do you offer custom plans?
Yes, our Enterprise tier is fully customizable. We'll work with you to design a plan that fits your specific needs, including custom integrations, dedicated support, and more.
What kind of support do you provide?
All plans include email support. Growth and Enterprise plans include priority support with faster response times. Enterprise also includes a dedicated account manager.
