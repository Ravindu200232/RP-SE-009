FAQ

Questions.

What makes NOIR different from other design studios?

We practice radical restraint. No colors to hide behind, no trends to chase. Every design decision is intentional and defensible.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects take 4-8 weeks from kickoff to launch. We move fast because we make decisions quickly and commit to them.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Absolutely. We work with clients across 12 countries and 4 continents. Time zone differences are never a barrier.

What if I need a design that uses color?

We'll tell you if you don't need it. But if color is truly essential to your brand, we'll integrate it with the same discipline we apply to black and white.

Can you work with our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. We'll audit your existing guidelines and either refine them or rebuild them from the ground up, depending on your needs.

What does the onboarding process look like?

We start with a deep-dive discovery session, followed by a brand audit, and then a clear roadmap with milestones and deliverables.
