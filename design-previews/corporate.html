// FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Corporate design system?
Corporate is a comprehensive design system for enterprise applications. It includes design tokens, reusable components, and guidelines for creating consistent, accessible, and scalable user interfaces.
How does AI personalization work?
Our AI engine analyzes user behavior and preferences to automatically adjust layouts, prioritize content, and suggest workflow optimizations. This creates a personalized experience for each user while maintaining brand consistency.
Is the design system WCAG compliant?
Yes, all components are built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We include keyboard navigation, screen reader support, high contrast modes, and reduced motion support out of the box.
Can I customize the design tokens?
Absolutely. All design tokens are exposed as CSS variables, allowing you to easily customize colors, typography, spacing, and other design decisions to match your brand while maintaining system consistency.
What kind of support is included?
All plans include access to our documentation and community forum. Pro and Enterprise plans include priority email support with guaranteed response times. Enterprise customers get a dedicated support engineer.
