Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about AntDesk.

How long does it take to set up?

Most teams are up and running in under an hour. Our 200+ connectors make it easy to connect your data sources quickly.

Can I migrate from my current data stack?

Yes! We provide migration tools and a dedicated migration team for enterprise customers. Most migrations take less than a week.

What security certifications do you have?

We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA compliant. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes! The Pro plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

Can I use AntDesk with my existing BI tools?

Absolutely. AntDesk integrates with Tableau, Power BI, Looker, and all major BI tools. We also offer a REST API and JDBC driver.
