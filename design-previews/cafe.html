Frequently asked

Is café really free for commercial use?

Yes, the core system is MIT licensed. You can use it in commercial projects without restrictions.

Can I customize the color tokens?

Absolutely. All colors are exposed as CSS variables — override them in your theme.

Do you provide Figma files?

Pro and Enterprise plans include the full Figma library, updated regularly.

How does accessibility testing work?

Every component is tested against WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard navigation and contrast.
