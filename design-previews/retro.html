Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Retro.

Is Retro really free to use?

Yes! The Starter plan is completely free forever. It includes all core components and is perfect for personal projects. Upgrade to Pro when you need more.

Can I customize the colors?

Absolutely! Every color is defined as a CSS custom property. Override them in your own stylesheet to match your brand perfectly.

Does Retro work with React?

Yes! Retro is framework-agnostic. It works with React, Vue, Svelte, or plain HTML. The components are just CSS and SVG.

How accessible is Retro?

Retro is built to WCAG 2.2 AA standards. All components have keyboard support, visible focus states, and proper ARIA attributes where needed.

Can I use Retro for commercial projects?

Yes! Retro is MIT licensed. Use it in commercial projects, client work, or anything else. Just keep the license file intact.
