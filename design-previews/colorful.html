Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Colorful.

What is Colorful?

Colorful is a comprehensive design system featuring vibrant, high-contrast palettes and gradients for creating engaging, memorable, and modern user experiences.

How do I get started?

Simply sign up for a free account and access our comprehensive documentation, component library, and Figma files to start building immediately.

Is Colorful accessible?

Absolutely! Every component is built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards, with keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.

Can I customize the design system?

Yes! Our token-based architecture makes customization easy. Override colors, typography, spacing, and more to match your brand perfectly.

What support do you offer?

All plans include access to our documentation and community. Pro and Enterprise plans include priority support with guaranteed response times.
