Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Fiction.

What is Fiction?
Fiction is a playful, accessible design system built for teams who want to create delightful digital experiences. It includes pre-built components, semantic design tokens, and comprehensive guidelines — all designed to be fun to use.
Is Fiction really free to start?
Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free forever. You get access to 5 components and basic tokens. Upgrade to Pro when you need more power and flexibility.
How does Fiction handle accessibility?
Fiction is built with WCAG 2.2 AA compliance from the ground up. Every component includes keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, and proper ARIA attributes. We test every release against accessibility standards.
Can I customize the design tokens?
Absolutely! All colors, spacing, typography, and other design tokens are fully customizable. Use our token system to match your brand while maintaining the playful Fiction aesthetic.
What support options are available?
Starter plan includes community support. Pro and Enterprise plans include priority support with guaranteed response times. Enterprise customers get a dedicated support engineer.
