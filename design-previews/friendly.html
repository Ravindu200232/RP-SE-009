FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Bloom.

Is Bloom really free to start?

Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free forever for up to 5 team members. No credit card required. You can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise whenever you're ready.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

How secure is my data?

We take security seriously. Bloom uses 256-bit encryption at rest and in transit, is SOC 2 Type II compliant, and undergoes regular third-party security audits.

Do you offer integrations?

Yes! Bloom integrates with Slack, GitHub, Figma, Google Drive, Microsoft Teams, and 50+ other tools. Our API also lets you build custom integrations.

What kind of support do you provide?

All plans include email support with a 24-hour response time. Pro and Enterprise plans get priority support with faster response times and dedicated success managers.
