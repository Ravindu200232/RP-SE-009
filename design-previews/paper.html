FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Paper?

Paper is a print-inspired design system for building accessible, beautiful web interfaces. It includes design tokens, components, and guidelines that ensure consistency across your products.

How do I get started?

Sign up for a free 14-day trial. You'll get access to the full component library, design tokens, and documentation. No credit card required.

Is Paper accessible?

Yes. Every component in Paper is built to meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards, including keyboard navigation, focus states, color contrast, and screen reader support.

Can I customize the theme?

Absolutely. Paper uses semantic design tokens for colors, spacing, and typography. You can override any token to match your brand while maintaining accessibility standards.

Do you offer support?

Yes. Starter plans include community support. Pro plans include priority email support. Enterprise plans include dedicated support with a 24-hour response time.
