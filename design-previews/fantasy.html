FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know

What platforms is Eldoria available on?

Eldoria is available on PC (Windows, macOS, Linux), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and mobile devices (iOS and Android) with full cross-platform play.

Is Eldoria free to play?

Yes! Eldoria is free to play with optional subscription tiers that unlock premium content, cosmetics, and quality-of-life features. The base game is completely free.

Can I play with friends?

Absolutely! Eldoria supports parties, guilds, and cross-platform multiplayer. You can team up with friends regardless of their platform.

How often are updates released?

We release major content updates every month, with seasonal events and balance patches in between. Our roadmap is public and community-driven.

Is there cross-platform progression?

Yes! Your character, progress, and purchases sync seamlessly across all platforms. Play on PC, then continue on your phone during your commute.
