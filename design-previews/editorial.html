FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What makes Editorial different from other publishing platforms?

Editorial is built on a foundation of typographic excellence and structured grids. Every component is designed with accessibility and editorial best practices in mind, giving you a platform that produces beautiful, readable content out of the box.

Can I migrate my existing content?

Yes. We provide migration tools and a dedicated migration guide that works with WordPress, Medium, Substack, and most other publishing platforms. Our team can also assist with complex migrations on the Enterprise plan.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

Is Editorial accessible?

Absolutely. Editorial is built to WCAG 2.2 AA standards. We use semantic HTML, keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, and screen-reader tested components. Accessibility is not an afterthought — it's a core design principle.

Can I customize the design?

Yes. Editorial uses a token-based design system. You can customize colors, typography, spacing, and layout through the theme editor. For advanced customization, custom CSS is available on Professional and Enterprise plans.
