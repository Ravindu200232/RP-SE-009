FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is risograph printing?

Risograph printing is a high-speed digital printing method that uses soy-based inks and master stencils. It's known for its vibrant colors, textured finish, and unique misregistration effects that give each print a hand-made quality.

How long does printing take?

Standard turnaround is 5 business days. Pro plans get 48-hour turnaround, and Studio plans can request 24-hour rush printing for urgent projects.

Can I use my own design files?

Absolutely! We accept PDF, AI, and PSD files. If you need help preparing your files or want us to design from scratch, our design support team is here to help.

What paper stocks do you offer?

We offer over 50 paper stocks, ranging from smooth coated papers to textured cotton papers. Popular choices include kraft, newsprint, and recycled cardstock.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! Orders over 5,000 prints qualify for volume pricing. Contact us for a custom quote and we'll work out the best rate for your project.
